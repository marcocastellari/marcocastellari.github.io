I'm an **Electronics & Software Engineer** who builds systems from the board up: PCBs and firmware at one end, C++ applications and data pipelines at the other, and the CI infrastructure that keeps both honest. What ties it together is a preference for code that is correct and predictable, and for pipelines that don't depend on "it works on my machine."

I care about **zero-cost abstractions, deterministic timing, and reproducible infrastructure**. Whether it's a header-only GPIO library that compiles down to a single instruction, an Active-Object framework that eliminates race conditions by design, or a Jenkins setup that rebuilds from scratch with one command - the goal is always the same: engineering that's clean, testable, and repeatable.

## What I Work On

- **C++ development** - modern C++ (17/20) for applications and libraries, with a focus on clean architecture, concurrency, and interfaces that are testable by design.
- **Embedded firmware** - real-time, event-driven systems on ARM Cortex-M (STM32), with an emphasis on deterministic behaviour and hardware-independent, unit-testable code.
- **Build systems & DevOps** - modern CMake architectures for cross-platform projects, containerised toolchains, and Configuration-as-Code CI/CD pipelines.
- **Hardware & PCB design** - a growing area: multi-layer for mixed-signal boards for with attention to signal integrity, power distribution, and EMI control.

## Featured Work

- **[STM32 Inertial Platform]({{< ref "/projects/stm32-inertial-platform" >}})** — a compact 4-layer IMU board that streams 6-DoF motion data over CAN, built for motorsport telemetry.
